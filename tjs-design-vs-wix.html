TJS Design vs. Wix: build it yourself, or have it built?

Short answerWix is a do-it-yourself builder with a monthly subscription (about $17–$39/month on annual plans). TJS Design is a done-for-you custom site, with plans from $0 down that include hosting, management and weekly Google posts. Choose Wix if you want to build and tinker yourself; choose TJS Design if you want a professional site connected to your Google profile without spending your own hours on it.

Both can work for a local business. Here's an honest look at cost, time, control and results.
