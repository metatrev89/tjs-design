How we source

Notes on these numbers.

  • We only publish numbers we can trace. Each stat links to the original study or a report that cites it directly.
  • Dates matter. The 56% and 84% figures are from 2017–2018 data. We keep them because they're the largest public breakdown of profile actions, and we label the year.
  • Surveys aren't guarantees. These figures describe consumers and businesses in aggregate. Your results depend on your market, reviews and competition.
