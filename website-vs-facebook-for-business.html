The setup we recommend

Use each one for what it's good at.

  1. Website = home base. Your services, service area, photos, reviews and contact info — linked from your Google Business Profile.
  2. Google Business Profile = how you're found on the map. Keep it updated and point its Website button at your site.
  3. Facebook = updates and community. Post your work, share news, and link back to your website.
