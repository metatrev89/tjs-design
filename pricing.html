TJS Design vs. an agency

Why it costs less than an agency.

Agencies do good work — but a big part of what you pay for is the structure around the work: account managers, project managers, discovery workshops, approval rounds and internal handoffs. That's why a small-business site from an agency commonly runs into the thousands and takes a month or more, and why ongoing changes get billed separately.

TJS Design is built for the opposite. It's one person — Trevor Spencer — doing the strategy, design, build, SEO and launch, then keeping the site and your Google profile current all year. You talk to the person building your site, on one 30-minute call.

When an agency is the better fit

If you need a large site with dozens of custom features, a full brand identity project, ongoing paid-ad management, or a team on retainer, an agency may be worth the premium. For a local service business that needs a fast, professional site connected to its Google profile, it usually isn't.
